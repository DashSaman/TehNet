# TehNet Business Search Brief

**Snapshot:** 2026-08-31  
**Market:** Persian-language users in Iran; remote services nationwide, on-site Tehran + nearby cities by arrangement.  
**Canonical domain:** `https://tehnet.ir`

## Business outcome

Search is not optimized for raw traffic. It must contribute to measurable commercial outcomes:

1. paid course purchase;
2. paid lab/file/script purchase;
3. monthly TehNet Pro purchase/renewal;
4. qualified networking-service lead;
5. hourly consultation request;
6. direct equipment order;
7. equipment price/availability inquiry.

The initial commercial ambition is to build the digital education/lab/script side toward at least ~60M toman/month before treating hardware volume as the only growth engine. This is a planning target, not a ranking or revenue guarantee.

## Audience / jobs to be done

### Network practitioner

Needs: solve a real MikroTik/Linux/Cisco/VoIP problem quickly, get a reproducible lab/script, deepen skill, buy the right tool/equipment when necessary.

### Beginner / career learner

Needs: a clear roadmap, prerequisite guidance, practical projects, affordable course steps rather than an expensive all-or-nothing bootcamp.

### Company / IT decision maker

Needs: a trustworthy provider who can diagnose, quote, implement, support and document the result remotely or on-site.

### Equipment buyer

Needs: model selection, compatibility/use-case guidance, current stock/price or clear inquiry flow, warranty/fulfilment facts and optional configuration.

## Initial topical priority

`MikroTik/VPN → Linux/Server → Cisco → VoIP → applied AI for Network/IT`

MikroTik is the initial wedge because it naturally connects education, scripts/labs, equipment and paid configuration/support.

## Differentiation requirement

A TehNet page should have at least one reason to exist beyond paraphrasing another site:

- first-party configuration/lab/script;
- tested topology or worked example;
- real command/config output;
- original troubleshooting decision tree;
- real product selection guidance tied to supported scenarios;
- real service process/pricing model/limitations;
- updated explanation for current RouterOS/tool versions;
- video + written instructions + downloadable asset in one durable page.

If none exists, keep the page in draft or do not create it.

## Conversion design

Primary loop:

`YouTube / Google → Tutorial → Lab/File → Course/Pro → Product → Service`

Do not force every step. Each page exposes only contextually relevant next actions.

### CTA hierarchy by page type

- Tutorial: download/use lab → course → relevant service.
- Lab: acquire file → Pro if eligible → tutorial/course.
- Course: buy course → relevant labs → equipment only if genuinely useful.
- Product: buy/inquire → selection content → optional configuration service.
- Service: submit qualified request → hourly consultation if appropriate → supporting proof/resources.

## Measurement plan

After launch connect GSC + analytics and track at minimum:

- non-brand organic clicks by page/intent cluster;
- tutorial → lab/course/service assisted conversions;
- organic digital-product revenue;
- Pro purchase/renewal from organic-assisted sessions;
- qualified service leads and lead-to-revenue rate;
- organic equipment revenue/inquiries;
- branded-query growth;
- indexed preferred URLs vs accidental/duplicate URLs;
- crawl/index/schema errors;
- AI-search referrals/citations only where measurable, never as a vanity claim.

## Baseline

TehNet is a new site. There is no valid historic TehNet GSC/organic revenue baseline yet. Initial baseline starts at launch; pre-launch claims about rank/traffic are not evidence.
