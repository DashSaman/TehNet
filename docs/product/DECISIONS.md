# TehNet Approved Product Decisions

**Source:** 20-question design session with the product owner on 2026-08-31.

1. **Market positioning:** comprehensive network brand; Learn, Lab, Services and Shop are all first-class.
2. **Revenue target direction:** build toward meaningful recurring monthly revenue from education/files/labs/scripts, with services/equipment as additional revenue.
3. **Monetization:** individual sales + monthly TehNet Pro.
4. **Content domains:** MikroTik/VPN, Linux/Server, Cisco, VoIP and AI.
5. **AI:** applied/project-oriented: admin tooling, automation, monitoring, troubleshooting, log analysis, AI helpdesk/network assistant.
6. **Visual mode:** dark + light; dark is anchor; derive visual continuity from the existing Tehran Network YouTube identity and modernize it.
7. **Homepage:** four equal primary paths: Learn, Lab, Services, Shop.
8. **Course pricing:** prioritize accessible and specialist tiers rather than expensive bootcamps at launch; working range ~300k–3m toman.
9. **Pro:** monthly plan at launch.
10. **Equipment inventory:** combination of direct available stock and inquiry price/availability for special items.
11. **Services:** priced standard packages + quote-based custom work + hourly consulting.
12. **Labs:** free + individually paid + Pro-only.
13. **Authentication:** email/password + Google login.
14. **Payments:** Iranian gateway first; code/data architecture should support future international providers.
15. **YouTube archive:** review old videos; keep/update useful videos, drop obsolete/weak ones; create useful supporting pages/assets/CTAs.
16. **Service coverage:** remote across Iran; on-site Tehran + nearby cities by arrangement.
17. **Brand/personality:** TehNet remains the independent primary brand; founder/instructor identity appears where it creates teaching/trust value without turning the whole site into a personal brand.
18. **Tone:** services/shop formal and trust-oriented; tutorials/labs technical, clear and friendlier.
19. **Support:** tickets + Telegram/WhatsApp + contact form/email; live chat later.
20. **Digital protection:** files use expiry/limited entitlement delivery; paid videos play in-site without normal direct-download CTA.
21. **Launch philosophy:** professional, sellable balanced MVP; do not delay launch for low-priority future features.

## Non-negotiable product loop

`YouTube / Google → Tutorial → Lab/File → Course/Pro → Product → Service`

Relationships are contextual; do not force irrelevant upsells.
